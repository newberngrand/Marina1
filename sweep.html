How to play

  • Tap a slip to open it. Your first tap is always safe and opens up a patch of the marina.
  • An opened slip shows how many crab pots are in the slips around it. A slip with no number has none, so its neighbors open on their own.
  • Neighbors are the slips touching it in its row, the slips straight and diagonally across its dock, and (with the box ticked) the nearest slips across the fairway on the next dock. Hover over or press and hold an opened slip to see which slips it counts.
  • Press and hold (or right-click) a slip to drop a buoy where you think a pot is. Or switch the button above the map to “Tap places buoys.”
  • Tap an opened number once all its pots are buoyed to open the rest of its neighbors in one go.
