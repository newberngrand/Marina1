    How to play

    • The game runs five days. Each day, pick one slip on the marina map to flip over a playing card.
    • Once someone picks a slip, nobody else can pick it that day. Every morning the cards are shuffled and dealt again.
    • Missed a day? Pick two cards the next day. Miss two days in a row and you're out.
    • On the last day, after your fifth card, you can trade up to 3 cards (4 if you keep an Ace) by picking more slips.
    • Miss the last day with four cards and you'll get one random card.
    • When the game ends, every hand goes on the leaderboard, best to worst. Ties go to high card and kickers, then to whoever finished first.
