Ballot

Votes have been cast, so the ballot choices are locked. You can still change the description, details and closing time.

Who can vote is set by the "Who can see this" boxes below: tick one group or several. Owners get one vote per slip. On the shared cruiser and staff logins (LTC, STC), each person types their name and gets one vote per name. Votes can't be changed once cast, and no votes are accepted after the closing time. Because cruisers vote by name, results are shown as a share of all votes cast.

Ineligible slips

Slips listed here can't vote on this ballot and aren't counted in the totals. Type one or several slips (separated by commas or spaces), pick the reason, and click Add.

Marina Documents

Upload Document

Board Documents are only visible to board members (on the Board page) and admins. They are never public.

Each reviewer gets an email with a private link. They can open the document without logging in, then approve it, ask for changes, or leave a comment. Their answers show up under Out for Review. The file isn’t added to Marina Documents.

New or replaced meeting minutes stay hidden from members until every board member approves them on the Board page.
